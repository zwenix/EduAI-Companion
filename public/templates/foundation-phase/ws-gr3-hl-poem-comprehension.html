Before the sun is fully awake,

the taxi rank is already wide awake:

a hooter goes beep, a taxi toots,

soldies shout "hup! hup!" in their street shoes.

A big yellow taxi, busy as a bee,

carries my schoolbag and my book and me.

Nomsa counts her change, one coin, two coins —

the meter is ticking, the morning is in a hurry.

Word study · Funda
  1. hooter — What does the hooter do? Say the sound like the taxi.
  2. soldier / soldie — Who sells things at the taxi rank?
  3. meter — Where do you hear a meter ticking?
  4. busy as a bee — Is that true or just a comparison? Explain.

💡 Teaching tip — before the questions

  • Read the poem aloud yourself first with exaggerated rhythm; Grade 3 learners copy your phrasing.
  • Let learners mark their own rhyme pairs in pencil with coloured highlighting before they write full answers.
  • For learners who read slowly, pair them for "echo reading" — you read a line, they read it back.
