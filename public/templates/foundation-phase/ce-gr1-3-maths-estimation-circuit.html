Physical

Blow the whistle every 5 minutes. Two groups may swap places while walking — that is your transition time.

  1. 1👥 Groups of 4 sit at their station. One "Reader", one "Recorder", one "Counter", one "Reporter".
  2. 2🤔 Estimate FIRST — no counting allowed until the estimate is written.1 min
  3. 3📏 Count / measure and record.3 min
  4. 4Whistle → move one table to the right.30 sec
  5. 5🎤 Reporters share their surprise result with the class.5 min

🏃 Space: 6 tables or floor spots around the room

💡 Teaching tip — estimation talk

  • Refuse "I don't know": the rule is "give a number that is not zero". Confidence first, accuracy second.
  • Grade 1 records with a tally and a number only; Grade 3 adds the "difference" column — same sheet, different demand.
  • Reuse the class graph data for the next week's informal Data Handling task — CAPS wants the same data revisited.
