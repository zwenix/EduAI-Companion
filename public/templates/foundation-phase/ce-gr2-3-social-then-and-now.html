🤝 Inclusive education

  • Some learners live with grandparents or in child-headed households — never assume a parent is available; the interviewer may be any older person.
  • For learners who cannot write, allow a drawn timeline and an oral report recorded on the teacher's phone.
  • Avoid romanticising poverty: CAPS Social Sciences in the Foundation Phase is about change and fairness, so ask "who had to fetch water, and how has that changed for your family?"
