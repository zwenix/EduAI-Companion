💡 Teaching tip — praise the work, not the child

  • Name the behaviour ("you checked your subtraction by adding back") so other learners copy the strategy, not just the result.
  • Give every learner at least one slip per fortnight; in Foundation Phase the fastest learners soak up all the airtime.
  • For Grade R, pair the slip with a stamp on the hand — the paper alone means very little at that age.
